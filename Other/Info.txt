Main abilities (If set keybinds for this is too difficult or complex, perhaps have a singular button that rotates through the abilities with labels for each one?) 
Green Light – The ring can produce a green light similar to a flashlight.
Protective Bubble – Create a green energy bubble around yourself, and potentially target another player you're pointing at. Ideally toggleable while the ring is selected. (Blocks from outside damage - turrets, mobs, mines, etc - Ideally a quick cast time for emergency use)
Energy Construct – Create a temporary green energy wall/structure (some transparency would be ideal). It would disappear after a certain amount of time or once the ring runs out of charge. (Maybe adjustable duration in mod config settings?)
Energy Pulse/Beam – A green energy blast that knocks enemies back and/or deals damage. Damage could be adjustable in the config.
Hover/Fly – Allow the player to briefly hover/slow their falling, mainly to prevent fall damage. It doesn't necessarily need to be full flight. (If you can add flight, that would be cool - but not absolutely necessary if difficult to implement)
Simple energy cage/trap - briefly slows or holds an enemy (Think a cage / dome around the target similar to the bubble earlier, but for hostile mobs to restrict movement - Maybe you could adjust the bubble to scale to size of target, and have it work on self/allies/hostiles so it would cover both use cases
Directional energy shield - Holds up a shield in front of you that moves with your character (Blocks damage from turrets)

Abilities should have a green color to it (similar to the rings color if not exactly the same) 

I'm more interested in having a handful of abilities that feel good and polished rather than trying to make the ring overly complicated.
(3/3)
Ring/Mod Configuration

Ideally, I'd like configurable options for: (if there's anything obvious I'm missing, feel free to add or let me know)

Ring scrap value
Ring spawn weight
Which moons the ring can spawn on (all moons option available please)
Whether the ring can be used by everyone or restricted to specific Steam IDs
Ability damage
Ability cooldowns
Ring energy/charge capacity/ability usage drain adjuster (If we're gonna go with a charge system at all, maybe have the default recharging station work on the ring as well? - Similar to regular flashlights)
Ability duration where applicable

I'd also like the ring to have some sort of charge/energy system, so the stronger abilities can't simply be spammed indefinitely. The exact recharge/consumption system can be whatever is easiest to implement.

I'm open to suggestions as well. If certain abilities are considerably more complicated than others, I'm happy to prioritize the simpler ones and potentially add more functionality later.
